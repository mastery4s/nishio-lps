Is lithium right for your operation?

Sometimes no. Here is how we actually decide with a customer, before anyone talks about price.

Your situationWhat we usually recommend
Indoor, two or three shifts, no battery roomEP lithium. Charge during breaks, no swapping, no ventilation
Indoor, single shift, existing lead-acid works fineLithium pays back more slowly. Worth a calculation, not an automatic yes
Outdoor yard, containers, rough groundEnforcer diesel. Electric is the wrong tool here
Short project, uncertain durationRent first. Buy after you know the real usage
Tight capital, steady long-term needUsed ex-rental, or long-term rental
What we can tell you that a pure seller cannot

We put electric machines on rental contracts and get them back. We see what the battery is like after three years, what fails, and what it costs to put right. When we say a lithium machine suits your operation, that is based on machines we have had returned to us — not on a specification sheet.

What changes with lithium

  • No battery change — charge during breaks instead of swapping
  • No battery room, no ventilation, no acid handling
  • No watering, no equalising charge
  • Consistent power until the charge runs out, instead of fading through the shift

What to check before you commit

  • Where the charger goes, and whether your supply can take it
  • Whether your break pattern gives enough charging windows
  • Battery warranty terms and what happens at end of life
  • Who services it locally — and how fast they get to you
