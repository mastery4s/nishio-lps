Buyer's guide

Five questions to ask before you buy a used forklift

Ask these to any seller, including us. A good machine at a fair price should survive all five.

01

How many hours has it run, and can you prove it?

The hour meter alone is not proof. Ask what records exist behind the number.

Our answer: most of our machines ran on our own rental contracts, so the hours come with our job records.

02

What has been repaired or replaced?

“Fully serviced” means nothing without a list. Engine, hydraulics, mast, chains — ask which parts and when.

Our answer: we can show the repair history, because our own workshop did the work under ISO 9001.

03

How was it used before?

A machine that lifted paper in a warehouse is not the same as one that ran on a wet yard for eight years.

Our answer: we know the site and the type of work, because we placed the machine there.

04

Who repairs it after I buy it, and how fast?

This is the question most buyers ask too late. A cheap machine with no service behind it is expensive.

Our answer: our own technicians and four branches — Shah Alam, Penang, Johor, Bintulu.

05

Are the spare parts in stock?

Waiting three weeks for a part means three weeks of stopped work.

Our answer: we stock parts for these models already, because we run them ourselves.
